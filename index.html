What data Solvo stores from Facebook

When you connect Facebook or Instagram inside Solvo, we store only what the connection needs to work:

  • Your Facebook name, email and user ID
  • The Facebook Pages you manage (name and fan count)
  • Linked Instagram Business/Creator account metrics
  • An encrypted access token (AES-256-GCM, never plaintext)

We never post to your accounts, and we never sell or share your data with third parties.

How to delete your data

Option 1 — Remove Solvo from your Facebook account (instant)

  1. Open facebook.com/settings → Apps and Websites
  2. Select Solvosys
  3. Tap Remove and confirm

Facebook immediately notifies us and we delete your stored Facebook and Instagram data automatically.

Option 2 — Disconnect inside Solvo

In the Solvo app: Profile → Connected accounts → select Facebook or Instagram → Disconnect. This deletes the stored token, profile and metrics from our servers right away.

Option 3 — Request by email

Email vikas.shevale@yahoo.com with the subject “Data Deletion Request” and the email address on your Facebook account. We process every request within 30 days and reply with a confirmation code.

What gets deleted

  • Facebook / Instagram access tokens
  • Your name, email and Facebook user ID
  • Page fan counts and Instagram account metrics

Deleting removes everything Facebook-related we hold about you. Solvo account data that is not tied to Facebook (campaign history, wallet, earnings) is handled separately — mention it in your email if you want that deleted too.
